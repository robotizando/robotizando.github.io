Events

Conferences, workshops, hackathons and science fairs I have taken part in as a speaker, workshop host or mentor — and often as track coordinator, competition organiser or judge.

A decade of them, from 2007 to 2018, spread across Brazil from Foz do Iguaçu to the middle of the Amazon, plus one trip to Las Vegas. Mostly free robotics, Arduino, 3D printing and the Internet of Things — taught hands-on, with open hardware and open tools. Each card groups every edition of the same event, with the years I was there.
