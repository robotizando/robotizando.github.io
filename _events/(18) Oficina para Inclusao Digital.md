# Oficina para Inclusão Digital

11th edition · 2012

The Oficina para Inclusão Digital is Brazil's national gathering for the digital
inclusion field — public-sector programmes, telecentres, community projects and the
researchers who study them.

Spoke on open and free technologies as a path to real digital inclusion: the difference
between handing someone a device and giving them the ability to understand, modify and
repair it.
