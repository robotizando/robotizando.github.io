# Campus Party Brasil

São Paulo · 2008–2017 · 8 editions

Campus Party is the longest-running thread in my speaking history. What started as
attending in 2008 turned into designing and curating an entire hands-on robotics area:
the **Oficina de Robótica Livre** (Free Robotics Workshop) and the **Desafio de Robótica
Livre** (Free Robotics Challenge), a line-follower competition built entirely on open
hardware and open tooling.

Across the editions the content followed the technology: from bare Arduino and homemade
Severino boards, through 3D printing, to ESP8266 and wearable PCBs.

## 2017

Two workshops in the official programme:

- [Esqueça o Arduino: desvendando o ESP8266](http://campuse.ro/events/campus-party-brasil-2017/talk/esqueca-o-arduino-desvendando-o-esp8266/)
- [Faça a sua própria placa de circuito eletrônico — Wearables](http://campuse.ro/events/campus-party-brasil-2017/talk/faca-a-sua-propria-placa-de-circuito-eletronico-wearables/)

Also took part in a panel alongside Newton C. Braga. Designed and 3D-printed custom
badge holders for the team.
