# UFOPA — Universidade Federal do Oeste do Pará

Santarém, Pará · 2014

Santarém sits at the meeting of the Tapajós and the Amazon, a long way from the usual
conference circuit — which is exactly why this one mattered.

Ran a parametric 3D modelling workshop using **OpenSCAD**, with everything designed and
printed on site. Participants produced their own pieces during the session: a modelled
sword, a picture frame with a separate locking part, custom lettering and keychains, all
built from a shared component library written live during the workshop.
