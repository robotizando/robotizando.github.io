# Latinoware

Foz do Iguaçu, Brazil · 2011–2017 · 7 editions

Latinoware is where my involvement went from *giving a talk* to *building the programme*.
From 2014 onwards I coordinated the **Education & Robotics** track: assembling the
schedule, inviting and onboarding speakers, and designing the physical layout of the
free-robotics / maker room.

## 2017

Invited speaker.

## 2016

Workshop with registered participants; 3D-printed parts produced for the event.

## 2015

Track coordination. Designed the free-robotics / maker workshop room in Sweet Home 3D,
producing floor plans and perspective renders used to brief the organisers.

## 2014

Track coordination for Education & Robotics — invitation letters, speaker photos and
the full session grid. Ran a JabutiEdu workshop with its own bill of materials.

## 2013

Talk and demo: line-following robot driven by LDR sensors and Arduino.

## 2012

Line-follower workshop and competition. Designed and produced the official track
artwork (1.5 m × 1.5 m practice track and 3 m × 3 m competition track) and the kit
packaging.

## 2011

First edition — talk *"O que é Arduino"*, covering the platform from the ground up.
