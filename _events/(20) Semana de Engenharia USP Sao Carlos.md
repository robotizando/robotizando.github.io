# Semana de Engenharia — USP São Carlos

18th edition · Integra Elétrica · São Carlos, São Paulo

Hands-on Arduino workshop at the Engineering Week of the University of São Paulo's São
Carlos campus, part of the Integra Elétrica programme.

Content covered:

- **PWM** — pulse-width modulation for analogue-style output
- **Knob** — reading a potentiometer and mapping analogue input
- **Ponte-H** — H-bridge motor driving, forward/reverse and speed control
