# IFG — Instituto Federal de Goiás

Goiânia and Luziânia, Goiás

Long-running relationship with the Federal Institute of Goiás, tied to the
[Metabotix](/projects/metabotix) open technologies laboratory at the Luziânia campus.

## Robot controller board

Designed a deliberately minimal controller for classroom robots, cheap enough to be
assembled by students:

- **ATtiny85** — 8-pin Atmel microcontroller, 6 usable I/O pins
- **L293D** — dual H-bridge, 2 pins per motor, 4 pins per robot
- **7805** — linear voltage regulator

The naming brainstorm for the board produced *Pequi-nino*, *Gyn-duino*, *Gyrino*,
*Ardu-gyno*, *GB-ino*, *Sumoduino* and *Just-Do-Ino*.

## Goiânia workshops

Multi-day free robotics workshop with full component, tooling and consumables planning
per group, plus the Xduino board design files and an accompanying talk on free robotics.
