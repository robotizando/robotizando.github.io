# IF Macaé

Macaé, Rio de Janeiro · 2013

Invited talk at the Federal Institute campus in Macaé, covering free robotics and the
**Xduino** — an Arduino-compatible board designed to be built and repaired locally.

Demonstrations shown during the session:

- Xduino and its shield ecosystem
- Mobile Xduino platform
- Line-following robot
- X-Caterpillar tracked platform
- X-Dimmer — AC phase-control dimmer
