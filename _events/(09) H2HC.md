# H2HC — Hackers to Hackers Conference

São Paulo, Brazil · 2016–2018 · 3 editions

H2HC is the oldest hacking and security research conference in Latin America. My
contribution sat where hardware hacking meets fabrication: designing and printing the
physical objects the conference hands out.

## 2018

Attendance and registration for the team.

## 2017

Designed and printed a set of personalised badge holders for the crew.

## 2016

Designed the **RTFM** piece — modelled in OpenSCAD, vectorised, and printed in several
colour variants — along with H2HC-branded objects and printed material.
