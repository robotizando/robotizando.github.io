# Arduino Day

Instituto Mauá de Tecnologia, São Paulo · 2016

Arduino Day is celebrated on the same weekend worldwide by communities running their own
local editions. The 2016 edition at Instituto Mauá de Tecnologia included talks, demos
and hands-on space for the maker community.

Presented on Arduino and 3D printing, with live printing during the event.
