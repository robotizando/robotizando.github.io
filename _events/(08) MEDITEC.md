# MEDITEC — UTFPR Medianeira

Medianeira, Paraná, Brazil · 2014–2016 · 3 editions

MEDITEC is the technology week at the Federal University of Technology – Paraná,
Medianeira campus. Three years running as an invited speaker.

## 2016

Talk: ***Hackeando o Arduino*** — stripping away the abstraction and working directly
with the underlying silicon. Covered the ATmega328 and the ATtiny25/45/85, compiling and
flashing raw `.hex` firmware without the Arduino IDE in the way.

## 2015

Talk with live demonstrations, including ferrofluids, and material on RS-485 wiring
guidelines for industrial serial communication.

## 2014

First edition as invited speaker.
