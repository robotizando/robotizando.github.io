# iMasters 7Masters

São Paulo, Brazil · May 2016

7Masters is iMasters' lightning-talk meetup: seven speakers, seven minutes each, all on
a single theme for the night. The May 2016 edition was dedicated to **3D printing**.

**Talk:** *Como construir sua própria impressora 3D* (How to build your own 3D printer) —
seven minutes to cover what actually matters when you build a printer from parts instead
of buying one. The session was recorded and published by iMasters.
