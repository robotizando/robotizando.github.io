# FGSL — Fórum Goiano de Software Livre

Goiânia, Brazil · 2014–2017 · 4 editions

## 2017

Designed and 3D-printed the trophies for the FGSL Capture the Flag competition — a
modelled Tux holding a flag, produced in several colour variants.

## 2016

Workshop and talk. Designed and printed conference badges for the event.

## 2015

Workshop *"Construa sua impressora 3D"* (Build your own 3D printer), accompanied by a
written article on the workshop methodology.

## 2014

Workshop: ***Nós vamos fazer Retro-Gaming usando Raspberry Pi e Arduino***

> Esta oficina apresenta um magnífico caso de uso, onde todo poder de uma placa da
> "Internet das Coisas" é unido ao "estado-da-arte" das plataformas de prototipagem
> eletrônica.

A use case joining an Internet-of-Things board with a state-of-the-art electronics
prototyping platform to build a working retro-gaming console.
