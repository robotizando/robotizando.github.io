# SEBRAE Roraima

Roraima, Brazil · 2013

Workshop hosted by SEBRAE in Roraima — Brazil's northernmost and least populous state,
and one of the hardest places in the country to reach with this kind of content.

Participants built **line-following robots** from scratch. Designed and produced the
practice and competition tracks for the session, printed locally from the source
artwork.
