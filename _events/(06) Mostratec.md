# Mostratec

Novo Hamburgo, Brazil · 2012–2016 · 4 editions

Mostratec is one of the largest international science and technology fairs for
secondary-school students in Latin America. My involvement was on the competition and
judging side rather than the stage.

## 2016

Judge for the **Prêmio Maker** (Maker Award) — reviewed the assigned projects against
the official evaluation rubric across the first stage of judging.

## 2014

Free Robotics Challenge.

## 2013

Ran the Free Robotics Challenge with registered teams and handled the awards ceremony.

## 2012

Built and ran the challenge arena, including the scoring back end for the
line-following event.
