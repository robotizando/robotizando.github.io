# FISL — Fórum Internacional de Software Livre

Porto Alegre, Brazil · 2012–2016 · editions 13 through 17

FISL was the free software conference in Brazil, and it hosted five straight years of
robotics and hardware content from me — a mix of stage talks and hands-on workshops.

## 2016 — FISL 17

Two sessions in the official grid:

- **Talk:** *Internet das coisas LIVRES com sucata e OpenWRT* — building IoT devices out
  of scrap consumer hardware reflashed with OpenWRT. Education & Robotics track.
- **Panel:** *A invasão dos drones: conectados, inteligentes e auto-replicantes*, with
  Christiane Borges Santos, Lays Rodrigues and Rodrigo Troian. Emerging Topics /
  Internet of Things track.

## 2015 — FISL 16

3D printing demos, including printing in chocolate.

## 2014 — FISL 15

Talk and workshop; covered by the event's official photography team.

## 2013 — FISL 14

Talk and hands-on demos.

## 2012 — FISL 13

Robotics workshop with registered attendees, plus a video piece produced for the talk.
