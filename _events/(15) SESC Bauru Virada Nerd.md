# SESC Bauru — Virada Nerd

Bauru, São Paulo · July 2015

Virada Nerd is SESC Bauru's weekend-long celebration of geek culture — games, comics,
science and technology, open to the general public.

Ran the maker and robotics activities across the weekend. Audiences at SESC are families
and curious passers-by rather than developers, which makes it one of the more useful
formats for actually widening who gets to touch this technology.
