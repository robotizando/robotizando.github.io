# Winter Challenge

2007

Winter Challenge was one of Brazil's established robot combat tournaments. This is where
the whole thing started for me — 2007, on the organising side rather than behind a
podium.

Contributed to the competition rules and operating procedures, the *"Livre-se de suas
tralhas"* side event regulations, and the project planning (work breakdown and SWOT
analysis) for the team's entry.
