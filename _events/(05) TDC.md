# TDC — The Developer's Conference

São Paulo (2015, 2016) · Porto Alegre (2016) · Florianópolis (2017)

I coordinated TDC's **3D Printing track**, which meant owning the whole thing: the
track pitch, the speaker line-up, and the live demos.

> In this track participants are introduced to the latest developments, applications and
> solutions in 3D printing, in a practical and objective way. Plenty of hands-on
> activities alongside networking with well-known professionals from the segment.
>
> — track description, TDC 2016 São Paulo

## 2017 — Florianópolis

Track coordination.

## 2016 — Porto Alegre

Wearables session: 3D-printed glasses driven by Adafruit NeoPixel LEDs, modelled in
OpenSCAD and printed on site.

## 2016 — São Paulo

Track coordination and live printing demos.

## 2015 — São Paulo

First edition as track coordinator. Ran calibration demos — bridging, brim and support
test prints — to teach print quality troubleshooting hands-on.
