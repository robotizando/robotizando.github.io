# Intel IoT Roadshow

São Paulo, Brazil · 2014, November 2015, March 2016

Intel's IoT Roadshow was a hackathon format: teams arrive with an idea, get handed an
Intel Edison, and have to ship a working prototype before the clock runs out. I worked
as a mentor and instructor — mostly meeting teams at the exact point where the hardware
refuses to cooperate.

## March 2016

Mentoring and hands-on support.

## November 2015

Mentoring and hands-on support.

## 2015

Wrote and delivered the bring-up walkthrough used by the teams: flashing the Edison
firmware image, connecting the dual USB cables, reaching the debug serial console at
115200 baud on `/dev/ttyUSB0`, and mounting and reflashing the on-board filesystem —
including the USB 3.0 gotcha that silently breaks the mount.

## 2014

First edition as mentor.
