# FLISOL — Festival Latinoamericano de Instalación de Software Libre

Guarulhos (2016) · Espírito Santo (2018)

FLISOL runs on the same day across hundreds of cities in Latin America, each edition
organised locally by its own community.

## 2018 — Espírito Santo

Organisation side: drafted the sponsorship request template used to approach local
companies.

## 2016 — Guarulhos

Talk and 3D printing demos at IF Guarulhos. Printed letter-shaped pieces for the
event's visual identity.
